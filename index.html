About Our School

The School of Pure and Applied Sciences (SPAS) at Mama Ngina University College is dedicated to academic growth, scientific exploration, technology and innovation.

Through our departments, we aim to provide learners with knowledge, practical skills and opportunities to explore solutions to real-world challenges in science, technology, health and the environment.

We strive to foster a learning environment that encourages curiosity, critical thinking, research and lifelong learning.
